HH-0.4.1 is the latest version of the long run and is retained. Open problems, most consequential first:

1. Time conversions between zones are wrong unless the person stops to work them out, and in a group they rarely do. Candidates: a world-clock capability in the body, or a habit that triggers on any time mentioned for another place.
2. Follow-through on commitments fails at the moment of acting: twice a person woken by their own alarm took the intention, or the alarm itself, for the deed. Candidate: the alarm's wake starts from the mind note's promise, and the hands see that nothing has been sent yet.
3. People state what they will do as already done ("i set a reminder") before the hands act.
4. The outward voice stays vivid on the third-person page but converges under shared creative tasks; HH-0.3.0's first-person transcript was more grounded and blander.
