# When you return: HH-0.2.0

HH-0.2.0 is retained. Read [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) for its runs, [BLUEPRINT.md](BLUEPRINT.md) for its design and the sibling HH versions for what came after. Place `Human HH-0.2.0` from the `holodeck.humans` library to talk to one; give it a name and leave the personality empty to have a life drawn for it. Its prompts are edited through the `humans-prompts` MCP server (version HH-0.2.0).
